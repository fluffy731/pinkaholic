Questions

Good to know

How long does delivery take?

Each order is printed for you and then shipped with tracking. Most orders arrive in about 14 days, and we show an estimated date before you pay. We'll email you a tracking link as soon as your order is on its way.

What can I print?

Up to two lines of 14 characters, a logo, or both, in wine, black, white or gold foil. Please only upload logos you have the right to use, and no offensive text. We'll contact you if we can't print something.

What logo file should I send?

A PNG with a transparent background prints most cleanly. SVG, JPG and WebP also work. Simple, bold logos read best on felt; fine detail and very thin lines may soften. We check every logo and email you before printing.

Are they real tennis balls?

Yes. They're standard-size pink felt tennis balls, lovely for a social hit, a backyard rally or a gift. They're designed for play and gifting rather than official tournaments.

Can my dog play with one?

They're made for people, not as pet toys. If your dog borrows one, please supervise and take it away once it's chewed. They're a sports and gift item, not a toy for children under 3.

Can I return a personalised ball?

Because every ball is printed for you, we can't take change-of-mind returns. If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or faulty, contact us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you. This doesn't affect your rights under Australian Consumer Law. See our returns policy.

Do you do larger orders for weddings, clubs or events?

Yes. For 24 balls or more, email us. Larger orders start from A$8.95 a ball, with no setup fee, and can have a different name on every ball.
