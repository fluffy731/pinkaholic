Shipping

We ship to every address in Australia and New Zealand. Each order is printed for you, then sent with tracking. Most orders arrive in about 14 days. We show an estimated delivery window before you pay. It's an estimate, not a guarantee, but if your order hasn't arrived 25 days after you placed it, contact us and we'll chase it, reprint it or refund you.

Shipping is included in every price, so there's nothing extra to pay at checkout. We'll email you a tracking link as soon as your order is on its way.

Returns & refunds

Every ball is printed with your own words, so we can't accept returns if you change your mind or ordered the wrong text. Please check your spelling before you pay.

If your order arrives damaged or faulty, or doesn't match the design you ordered, email us a photo within 30 days of delivery. We'll reprint it or refund you, whichever you prefer. Our goods come with guarantees that can't be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law, and nothing in this policy limits them. New Zealand customers keep their rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, delivery address, phone number and the text you want printed. We only use them to make, send and support your order. Your text and delivery details are shared with our print partner and couriers for that purpose only. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, and we never see or store your card number.

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your last ball design is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you come back. To see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, email us.

Contact

Email us and we'll reply within two business days.
