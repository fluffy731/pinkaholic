# Browser research: competitors, suppliers and trends

Researched 20 October 2026 in the owner's Chrome. Prices are as shown on each site that day. Alibaba was set to AUD.

## Summary

- **Personalised pink tennis balls barely exist in Australia.** Searches turned up one direct competitor (a new eBay seller in NSW with one sale) and one overseas Amazon listing with weak reviews.
- **Chinese suppliers print logos in bulk, not one name per order.** Blank or logo-printed pink balls cost about A$0.42–1.44 each, but nobody advertises printing a different name on every small order. That has to be confirmed with suppliers before launch (see the questions at the end).
- **The strongest demand signal is weddings and parties.** TikTok shows personalised tennis balls as wedding favours (often from "your furry family"), party favours, baby announcements and calligraphy/hot-foil gifts.
- **Our prices hold up.** We're cheaper than the eBay competitor once postage is counted, and dearer per ball than Amazon's 6-pack, which ships from overseas and rates 3.3★.

## TikTok trends

- **Wedding favours** are the biggest theme: "Gifted with love from your furry family" custom balls, DIY Cricut name balls, and "we love anything custom at a wedding". The top post had about 2,400 likes.
- **Party and housewarming favours**, and **baby announcements** ("customised tennis balls with the baby's name").
- **Premium finish:** calligraphy with hot-foil engraving on tennis balls.
- **Padel:** Namethatball (UK) sells personalised padel balls.
- **Brand activation:** American Express personalised balls at the US Open fan experience.
- Instagram needed a sign-in, so it wasn't checked.

## What I'd change (for the owner to decide)

1. **Add a wedding and party favours offer.** Price per ball with a minimum of 24, a name or date on each ball, and a gift tag. That's the clearest demand on social media. Our bulk tiers (A$8.95 / A$7.95 / A$6.50 per ball) fit between Pakistan's logo bulk (A$4.41–5.32) and retail.
2. **Consider a 6-pack at A$59.95** (A$9.99/ball) instead of A$69.95. Amazon's overseas 6-pack is A$48.56, but ours is pink, previewed and local. Keep the single at A$24.95 and the can of 3 at A$39.95: the eBay competitor works out at A$36.80 delivered.
3. **Think about printing in Australia.** Import blank pink balls at about A$1 each and print names locally (UV printer, pad printing or hot foil). Delivery would drop from about 14 days to 2–5, which removes our biggest weakness against the eBay seller. It needs equipment (cost not researched).
4. **Dog lovers are a big audience** (the "furry family" wedding trend), but our FAQ says the balls aren't pet toys. If we want that market, source a pet-safe ball and say so honestly.

## Questions to ask suppliers (launch board task 5)

1. Can you print a **different name on each ball, one order at a time**, and ship each order direct to a customer in Australia or New Zealand? What's the cost per order?
2. If not, what's the MOQ for blank pink balls, and for pink balls in a can of 3?
3. What print methods do you offer (UV, pad, heat transfer, hot foil), and how many colours and characters fit on the ball?
4. Is the felt pink all the way through, and is the ink non-toxic (EN 71 / AS/NZS ISO 8124 test reports)?
5. What's the shipping cost and time to Melbourne, Sydney and Auckland for 1 ball, 3 balls in a can, and 12 balls?
