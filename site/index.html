Good questions

How long does delivery take?

Each order is printed for you and then shipped with tracking. Most orders arrive in about 14 days, and we show an estimated date before you pay. We'll email you a tracking link as soon as your balls are on their way.

What can I print?

Up to two lines of 14 characters each, in one of three print styles and white or berry ink. Please don't use logos, brand names, other people's trademarks, or anything offensive. We'll contact you if we can't print something.

Are they real tennis balls?

Yes. They're standard-size felt tennis balls, great for a social hit, a backyard rally or a gift. They're designed for fun and gifting rather than official tournament play.

Can my dog play with one?

They're made for people, not as pet toys. If your dog borrows one, please supervise and take it away once it's chewed.

Can I return a personalised ball?

Because every ball is printed with your words, we can't take returns if you change your mind. If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or faulty, contact us within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund you. This doesn't affect your rights under Australian Consumer Law. See our returns policy.

Do you do bigger orders for clubs or events?

Yes. The club dozen suits most teams. For 50 balls or more, email us for a quote.
